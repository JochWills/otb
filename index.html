Welcome to On The Bay

We are perfectly situated in Summerstrand, just one block from Port Elizabeth’s popular beachfront. The beach is a hive of activity and hosts major water and sporting events — yet in the middle of our garden you mostly hear the waves.

Every room has its own entrance, an en-suite bathroom with a shower, a smart TV with DStv and Netflix, a bar fridge, toaster, microwave, and tea and coffee-making facilities. Some rooms have self-catering facilities for preparing a light meal.

The rooms are vibrantly furnished and individually decorated, and each one opens onto its own patio. Secure on-site parking is available. We can set up an individual braai for you, or you are welcome to use the shared braai and entertainment area.

Driving here

From the N2

Follow the Settlers Way (M4) signs to City, then to Summerstrand. Stay on the M4 all the way to the beachfront, even where the name changes from Summerstrand M4 to Humewood M4. Travel along the beachfront and turn right into 6th Avenue, just after the Marine Hotel (Protea). On The Bay is up the road, across Margate Street.

From the airport

Turn right into Alister Miller. At the traffic lights turn right and follow La Roche to the beachfront, with McDonald’s on your right-hand side. Turn right and travel along the beachfront, then turn right into 6th Avenue just after the Marine Hotel (Protea). On The Bay is up the road, across Margate Street.
