About the room

Made for longer stays

Set up for longer stays, with the facilities to put together a light meal without going out. It’s an open-plan room with a sofa, a writing desk, a TV and plenty of cupboard space.

The kitchenette has a sink, microwave, kettle and a Nespresso machine. A glass door leads out to a private walled courtyard with a table and deckchairs, and the en-suite has a shower.
